## Consent script (read, do not improvise)
> Thanks for the time. I work on [area]. I am trying to understand how [activity] actually
> works for you. I am not selling anything and there are no right answers. Skip anything
> you would rather not answer, and you can stop at any point. I would like to record so I
> can get your words right. The recording stays with the team, is used only for this
> research, kept for [period], and I can delete it if you ask. Is recording okay?

Consent captured at: [timestamp]

## Warm-up (3 min)
- Tell me about your role. What does a typical week look like?
- Who else is involved in [activity]?

## The story (20-25 min)
Opener: **Tell me about the last time you [specific activity].**

Probes to have ready:
- What happened next? What did you do just before that?
- Walk me through it step by step. What did you have open?
- How long did that take? How often does that happen?
- What did that cost you?
- How do you handle it today? Is there a workaround? Can you show me?
- Have you looked for a tool for this? What did you find?
- [three seconds of silence]

## Solution exposure (10 min, optional, ALWAYS LAST)
Options shown: [A, B] · Order for this participant: [A first / B first]
- Take a look and think out loud.
- Which of these, if either, fits how you work? Why?
- What would you use this for? Take me to a specific situation.
- What would it replace? What is missing?

## Close (3 min)
- What have I not asked about that I should have?
- Who else does this differently that I should talk to?
- Would you be open to a follow-up in a few months?

## Do not ask
Would you use it · How much would you pay · Do you like it · How important is X ·
What features do you want · Don't you think · Would it be helpful if
